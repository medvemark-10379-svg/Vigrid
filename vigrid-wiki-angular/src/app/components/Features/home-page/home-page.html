Vigrid: Survive the Ragnarök

Vigrid is a dark, tactical roguelike deckbuilder that fuses the addictive path-branching of Slay the Spire with the polished, high-stakes card mechanics of Hearthstone. Set against the bleak backdrop of Norse mythology and folklore, the game isn't just about winning—it’s a desperate struggle to endure the end of the world.

The Vision: Challenge Meets Mythology

The core goal of Vigrid is to provide a single-player experience that is as educational as it is challenging. Players aren't just fighting for high scores; they are embarking on a journey to truly understand Norse Mythology and the world-shattering events of Ragnarök. It is the perfect title for those fascinated by Viking culture or anyone who wants to step into the boots of a legendary Norse figure.

The Journey: Surviving the Ragnarök

Your goal is simple but brutal: survive the unfolding end of the world. You must navigate through a procedurally generated world, where every decision brings you closer to the heart of the conflict. In Vigrid, you are not just a traveler; you are a participant in the ultimate clash between gods and giants. Every encounter is a choice between gaining the strength to endure or meeting a cold, forgotten death.
